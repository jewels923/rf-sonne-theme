Follow the Production Path from Machining to RF Testing

Six steps describe how a drawing becomes a tested part. Each step names the object a buyer can check during an audit, so a visit can be organized around evidence instead of a tour route.

  1. Precision machiningDrawing-defined cavities, housings and interfaces on high-precision CNC equipment.
  2. Surface treatment coordinationPlating and surface requirements arranged to the drawing; specific processes and compliance documents are confirmed per project and audit.
  3. AssemblyAutomated equipment, fixtures and manual stations, with in-process checks at each stage.
  4. RF tuningVNA-based adjustment against the agreed response and acceptance limits.
  5. TestingBench and automated measurements, with records linked to the tested object and conditions.
  6. Volume assessmentProcess route, inspection plan and capacity review before a production schedule is discussed.

Factory Audit Questions and Next Steps

Five questions that come up before a visit, answered with the same boundaries used throughout this page. Each answer points to the route that starts the next step.

Can I audit the SONNE factory in person?

You can request an in-person factory audit through the Factory or Contact page. Include your company, product interest, audit objectives, preferred dates and the processes you need to review. SONNE will confirm availability, timing and accessible areas for the visit. A requested date is not confirmed until the team responds with the visit arrangements.

Can I request a remote factory review?

You can ask for a remote review and identify the machining, assembly or testing areas relevant to your audit. The team will confirm what can be presented and whether a suitable remote session can be arranged. For a useful review, send your audit checklist in advance and separate general capability questions from evidence needed for your specific project.

What equipment can I verify at the factory?

The factory content covers high-precision CNC machining, automated and manual assembly, VNA tuning and RF test areas. SONNE states that it has more than 50 high-precision CNC machines plus VNA, PIM, reliability and matrix automated testing capabilities. For an audit, request the equipment and records relevant to your product rather than relying only on a general equipment list.

Does annual capacity above 500,000 units guarantee my delivery date?

No. Annual capacity above 500,000 units is a factory-level capability figure, not a delivery guarantee for an individual order. Production timing depends on the drawings, quantities, processes, validation requirements and available capacity. Submit the actual project details so the team can assess a schedule; prototype and mass-production timing should be confirmed separately.

Is ±0.01 mm tolerance guaranteed for every RF component?

No. For structures above 40 GHz, mechanical tolerance can reach ±0.01 mm depending on the design and manufacturing requirements. It is not a blanket guarantee across every component or feature. Share the drawing and identify critical dimensions so engineering can assess feasibility and the appropriate inspection approach for your specific part.

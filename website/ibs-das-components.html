IBS and DAS Passive Component Questions

Five recurring questions about a complete BOM, model-level PIM, the line MOQ, 3 dB hybrid use and the boundary between this page and the RF combiners page.

Can SONNE support a complete DAS passive-component BOM?

Yes. Submit one BOM covering POI requirements, low PIM splitters, directional couplers and hybrid couplers, with model or function, frequency, power, PIM, connectors and quantities. Indoor antennas, loads and cable assemblies can be added through the RF accessories page. Each item is checked against its applicable datasheet rather than treated as one uniform specification.

Does every DAS component meet PIM of -160 dBc or better?

No. The published value of up to ≤-160 dBc applies only to specific models, not automatically to the entire IBS/DAS range. Check the selected model's datasheet, test conditions and required power level. For project approval, request the applicable PIM evidence and agree on verification coverage before ordering.

What is the MOQ for IBS and DAS passive components?

The source specification for this IBS/DAS line states MOQ 50+, and that limitation must not be applied to SONNE's other product lines. Confirm the exact model, quantity and order conditions with your BOM. Availability and production timing are reviewed per model and order rather than presented as a universal stock or delivery policy.

Where is a 3 dB hybrid coupler used in an in-building DAS?

A 3 dB hybrid coupler can split or combine signals while providing a defined phase relationship between ports, depending on the circuit and system design. In an in-building DAS, selection should consider frequency range, power, PIM, isolation, amplitude and phase balance, and connectors. Confirm these values on the specific model datasheet.

Should I use this page or the RF combiners page for my requirement?

Use this page for in-building DAS BOMs centered on low PIM splitters, directional or hybrid couplers and POI requirements. Use the RF combiners page for generic duplexers, diplexers, multiplexers, multi-band combiners or RF power dividers. If the system includes both, submit the band plan and BOM so each requirement can be routed correctly.

RF Accessory Questions for Replacement and Project Orders

Five questions asked before an accessory order: combined shipment, stock and MOQ, load replacement, custom cable assemblies and indoor antenna band coverage.

Can RF accessories be shipped together with the main components?

Include the accessories and main components in the same project BOM and identify which items should ship together. SONNE supports same-factory project matching, but consolidated shipment must be confirmed for the specific order, models, quantities and production schedule. Do not assume a common delivery date before order review.

Are RF accessories in stock, and what is the MOQ?

Stock and MOQ depend on the exact accessory model and order. SONNE does not publish one cross-product-line stock or MOQ policy for this accessories page. Send the model, quantity and required date for an order-specific review. Pricing and production or delivery timing are confirmed separately from the initial engineering response.

Can I replace a termination load using only the connector and power rating?

No. Connector type and power rating alone are not enough to confirm a replacement. Also check the original model, frequency range, impedance, VSWR, connector gender, mechanical limits and PIM requirement where applicable. Provide the datasheet or clear nameplate photographs so the proposed load can be reviewed against the original specification.

What is needed for a custom RF cable assembly inquiry?

Provide the cable type or performance target, frequency range, length, connector series and gender at both ends, orientation, quantity and installation constraints. Include electrical or environmental requirements when applicable. SONNE can review the assembly against these inputs; feasibility, pricing and delivery are confirmed for the exact configuration rather than inferred from a similar photograph.

Do all indoor DAS antennas cover 698–3800 MHz?

No frequency range should be applied to every indoor antenna without checking its model datasheet. Use the required 698–3800 MHz range as an RFQ filter, then confirm gain, VSWR, polarization, power, connector and mounting requirements for the selected antenna. The appropriate model depends on the complete DAS design and installation conditions.

Specify Outdoor Materials, Coating and Installation Conditions

Material and finish choices belong in the RFQ, not in a generic catalog claim. Describe the installation and the appearance requirement, then let engineering confirm which structure, material and coating route applies.

Material preference

State the preferred material family or the constraint behind it; the final grade is confirmed with the structure.

Color and coating

Give the target color and coating expectation, including whether the surface is visible to end users.

Installation environment

Indoor, outdoor, underground or coastal exposure, plus the maintenance access expected.

Dimensions

Available space, envelope limits and the mounting surface the enclosure will sit on.

Cable entries

Entry count, cable diameter range, entry direction and whether glands are supplied.

Appearance requirements

Branding area, label position and any restriction on visible fasteners or seams.

FTTX Distribution and Closure Questions

Five questions that come up before an FTTX quote: the IP boundary, OEM branding, outdoor finish, custom size and the evidence needed for a specific configuration.

What IP rating can SONNE FTTX products achieve?

IP68 is a published capability for splice-closure products, not a blanket rating for every FTTX box or cabinet. Other ratings must be confirmed against the exact enclosure design, cable entries and use conditions. Identify the product type and target rating in your RFQ, then request the applicable test evidence for that configuration.

Can FTTX enclosures be customized with OEM branding?

Yes, custom enclosures and OEM branding are supported for FTTX projects. Submit dimensions, installation interfaces, internal layout needs, cable entries, quantities and logo artwork. Branding method, color, placement and production requirements are confirmed per project, along with any drawing or sample approval needed before volume production.

How are outdoor corrosion and finish requirements handled?

Specify the installation environment, material preference, coating or color requirement, exposure conditions and expected maintenance constraints. SONNE has outdoor material and coating capabilities, but no universal corrosion duration, salt-spray rating or environmental compliance should be assumed. The applicable finish and any required evidence are confirmed for the exact project.

Can I request a custom FTTX box size or mounting arrangement?

Yes, custom enclosure requirements can be reviewed. Provide the available space, mounting method, cable-entry locations, internal distribution or splicing needs, target protection level and estimated quantity. Feasibility and production timing depend on the final structure, materials, process and capacity, so they are assessed after the requirement package is complete.

What should I include when requesting IP test evidence?

Identify the exact product category, model or drawing, target IP rating, cable-entry configuration and intended outdoor installation. Ask for evidence applicable to that configuration rather than a generic certificate. If the design is customized, confirm whether existing evidence remains applicable or whether additional project-specific verification is required.

07 / Selection notes5 questions

Custom Filter Selection and Manufacturing FAQs

Direct answers for structure selection, high-frequency feasibility, project timing, low-PIM inputs and technical documents.

How do I choose between cavity, waveguide and tuning-free filters?

Start with frequency, insertion loss, rejection, power, PIM, size and interface requirements. Cavity and waveguide describe different structural approaches, while tuning-free describes a manufacturing and adjustment objective rather than a mutually exclusive filter type. Engineering should compare the complete specification and production expectations before recommending an approach.

Can SONNE make E-band filters above 40 GHz?

Selected custom microwave and waveguide projects can reach up to 110 GHz, depending on topology and validation, so E-band requirements can be submitted for evaluation. This is not a universal limit for every filter design. Provide the exact passband, rejection, power, waveguide interface and mechanical requirements for a feasibility review.

How long do filter prototypes and mass production take?

The fastest prototype reference is about two weeks, but it is not a general lead-time promise. Actual prototype and production timing depends on topology, drawings, quantity, process and capacity. A complete RFQ typically receives initial engineering feedback in about 24 hours; formal quotation and delivery schedules require project-specific confirmation.

What is needed to evaluate a high-power, low-PIM filter?

Provide the frequency bands, insertion loss and rejection targets, power level and power type, PIM limit and test conditions, return loss, connectors, mechanical envelope and operating environment. Also state expected quantity and verification requirements. High power and low PIM must be assessed with the complete design and test setup, not as standalone promotional labels.

Can I request filter datasheets and S-parameter files?

You can request available datasheets, drawings and S-parameter files for the relevant filter model or custom project. Document availability and the level of detail depend on the product and project, so identify the model, frequency band and required file type. Controlled documents or project-specific information should be discussed during the engineering review.

What Happens after You Contact SONNE

Four steps describe the response. The approximately 24-hour guideline applies to initial engineering feedback on a complete RFQ; an incomplete message is used to clarify, not to quote.

  1. Received

    Your message reaches the engineering and sales team through the current inquiry route.

  2. Completeness check

    The team checks the application, product line, key requirements and attachments.

  3. Initial engineering review

    Feasibility questions, missing inputs and the applicable test scope are assessed.

  4. Reply with next step

    You receive clarifying questions or a proposed next step, such as a drawing review or sample discussion.

Six items for the first email

  • Application and operating context
  • Product line or component function
  • Key frequency or mechanical requirements
  • Quantity range or project stage
  • Available attachments and their revision
  • The question you need answered first

What is not assumed

Commercial terms, confidentiality arrangements and after-sales conditions are not set by a website page; they are confirmed for the specific project. If documents are sensitive, describe the confidentiality need before exchanging files.

Contact and Engineering Response FAQs

Five questions about reply timing, incomplete specifications, email, visits and messaging channels, answered without promising what cannot be promised.

How quickly will SONNE respond to my inquiry?

A complete RFQ can typically receive initial engineering feedback in about 24 hours. This is not a guarantee of a finished quotation, technical proposal or delivery schedule. If information is missing, the first reply may focus on clarifying the application and requirements. Timing for a formal quote and production is confirmed after the project has been reviewed.

Can I contact an engineer without complete specifications?

Yes. Start with your application, product line, known frequency or mechanical requirements, project stage and the question you need answered. Mark unknown values rather than guessing. The team can use that information to identify the next clarification steps. A complete RFQ is still needed before a reliable technical or commercial evaluation can be completed.

What email address should I use for an RF project?

Use info@zhide-tec.com for current RF, waveguide and enclosure inquiries. Include your company, application, product line and the main requirements in the subject or message. Attach available specifications or drawings when appropriate, and mention if confidentiality should be discussed before sensitive files are exchanged. The Contact form provides an alternative route.

How do I arrange a visit to the Ningbo facility?

Submit a factory visit request with your company, preferred dates, number of visitors, product interest and audit objectives. SONNE will confirm the schedule, visit scope and who will attend. The manufacturing address is published above, but confirm your appointment before travelling; a preferred date is not a confirmed booking.

Is WhatsApp available for contacting SONNE?

A public WhatsApp channel has not been confirmed for the website. Use the Contact form, email info@zhide-tec.com or call +86-0574-63615958 so your project details reach the team. Do not assume the published telephone number is a WhatsApp account.

Production Capabilities & Quality Control

  • Two-stage QC: PPS confirmation before mass production + 100% pre-shipment inspection on every order.

  • Batch testing: drop-ball impact, UV transmission and hinge-cycle testing on every production run.
  • Documented delivery: each export carton ships with its 100% inspection report, the approved PPS reference and CE/FDA documents.

Production Process: 10 Disciplined Steps

Every pair of Julong sunglasses moves through a disciplined production line inside our dust-free workshop. Each station is followed by an inline quality check, so defects are caught early, not at final inspection.

01

Molding

Heated TR90 or polycarbonate is injected into precision molds to form the base frame.

02

Painting

Frames are painted and coated for appearance, scratch and corrosion resistance.

03

Cutting

Lenses are cut to exact shape for a perfect fit with the frame.

04

Bending

Lenses are heat-formed to match the frame's base curve for optical comfort.

05

Laminating

Protective and functional films (mirror, hydrophobic, anti-fog) are laminated onto lenses.

06

Welding

Frame components are welded together for a sturdy, durable structure.

07

Stitching

Straps and soft parts are stitched with high-strength seams.

08

Assembling

Lenses, frames, hinges and straps are assembled into the finished product.

09

Testing

Every batch undergoes drop-ball, UV and hinge-cycle testing to meet standards.

10

Packaging

Approved sunglasses are packed securely in custom retail packaging for shipment.

Buyer FAQ

About Julong: Frequently Asked Questions.

Straight answers to the questions every buyer asks before placing an OEM/ODM order. Need more detail? Send us your question directly. We reply within one business day.

Is Julong a manufacturer or a trading company?

We are a direct manufacturer in Guangzhou, China, operating since 2011. Our two factories cover 8,000 m² with dust-free assembly lines and 105 in-house staff: sales, R&D and production under one roof. Nothing is outsourced. Video factory tours are welcome any business day.

What is your MOQ for custom sports sunglasses?

Our standard MOQ is 300 pieces per model per color. Trial orders below the MOQ are accepted with a 10% unit-price adjustment to cover machine setup costs.

How fast can you make samples?

Our R&D team returns structural suggestions within 24-48 hours of receiving your drawings or reference samples, and 3D prototyping is available for complex custom structures. A sample fee applies (PayPal accepted), with shipping freight borne by the buyer.

What are your payment terms?

Standard terms are a 30% deposit on order confirmation, with the 70% balance paid before shipment. Sample fees can be paid via PayPal.

How do you control quality during production?

Two stages: first, you approve a Pre-Production Sample (PPS) before mass production starts. Second, 100% of finished pieces are inspected before shipment, not a random AQL draw. Every carton ships with its inspection report plus CE/FDA documents.

Can you apply my brand logo and custom packaging?

Yes. Logo methods include silkscreen printing, laser engraving, etching, epoxy metal plates and metal trim, placed on the temple, lens corner, nose bridge, elastic strap or retail packaging. We also produce custom color boxes and hard cases, with frame colors matched to your Pantone codes.

How do you protect my design from being copied?

We hold 30+ proprietary design patents and sign NDAs before any design discussion. Around 90% of our output is exclusive OEM/ODM work: your molds, your specs, your product, never resold to other buyers.

What products and markets do you specialize in?

Sports sunglasses for cycling, running and outdoor use: TR90 frames, polarized and photochromic lenses, UV400 protection, adjustable nose pads and magnetic quick-change lens systems. We export mainly to North America, Europe, Australia and Southeast Asia.

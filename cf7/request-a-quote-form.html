Review Your RFQ and the Next Steps before Submitting

Check the summary, then submit the information you have. A complete RFQ typically receives initial engineering feedback in about 24 hours, but this is not a guaranteed firm quotation or delivery commitment. If details are missing, the first reply may focus on clarification.

What happens after submission

  1. SubmittedYou receive a confirmation with the details sent.
  2. Completeness CheckEngineering checks whether the inputs are enough for review.
  3. Engineering ReviewKnown requirements and open questions are assessed.
  4. Clarification or Next StepThe team replies with questions or the recommended next step.
